# I'm OK media repo

Before any marketing, social, blog or promotion work for I'm OK, read:
- PROMO_PLAN.md: the promotion plan (audiences, channels, budget, targets, schedule). The full doc with images: https://claude.ai/artifact/MGoaTi9EndTkoV9bcc56sU
- BRAND.md and PLAN.md: social media voice, pillars and calendar.
- Marketing ideas inbox (Google Doc, Enrico adds ideas there; sort them into its sections when planning): https://docs.google.com/document/d/1GFb_3Ns04XlIE3vGCa5lQA4m8adFfM1EZ-MXVxfH95Q/edit
- Audience playbook (one tab per audience: groups, subreddits, contacts, posts, ads): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/228a240e-1ba5-41d7-9338-a5a940b970e3
- Free growth plan and outreach checklist (doc with two tabs): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/b205941c-2cdd-4c5c-9896-a7efd7b89038
- CHARACTERS.md: the cartoon cast and style guide for comics and videos.
- BLOG.md and BLOG_PLAN.md: how the blog is written and what comes next.

Ad creatives and print pieces are in `ads/` (rendered by `tools/render-ads.js`).

Bot source code (private backup): https://github.com/enrico9797/imok-bot (src/index.js is the deployed Worker; build/p1..p19.js are the parts).
