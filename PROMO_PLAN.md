# I'm OK promotion plan (summary)

The full plan, with ad images and print pieces, is the doc "I'm OK promotion plan":
https://claude.ai/artifact/MGoaTi9EndTkoV9bcc56sU

Read this before any promotion work. Update it when something changes.

## The approach
- One audience at a time. **First: adults helping a parent** (35 to 60, including people abroad). **Second: people living alone.** Then couples and flatmates, parents of teenagers and students, shared goals (January).
- Free channels are the engine. The budget (about £25 a month; £50 would allow two tests at once) tests messages, it doesn't buy growth.
- Every page, ad and QR code has its own start code, so we know what works. Report: `/admin/sources?key=...`.

## What exists
- Audience pages: getimok.app/parents, /living-alone, /couples, /home-safe, /goals.
- QR short links: getimok.app/go/bin, /go/flyer, /go/poster (scans counted, then WhatsApp opens with "Start <code>").
- Ads (1080x1350 feed, 1080x1920 stories) and print (bin sticker, A6 flyer, A4 poster) in `ads/`. Renderer: `tools/render-ads.js`.
- Blog: three guides a week, automated (see BLOG.md, BLOG_PLAN.md). Social: daily posts, automated (see BRAND.md, PLAN.md).
- In the bot: one-message AI reminders (typed or voice note), a one-time "use it for your own people" line after someone's second Done.

## Free channels
- SEO blog and audience pages.
- Facebook groups: carers and ageing parents, Italians/Spanish in London, Chiswick and Hounslow community groups. Help first; ask admins before posting about the product.
- Reddit (r/AgingParents, r/CaregiverSupport, r/LivingAlone, r/london): help first, link only when asked. Enrico has zero karma: build it slowly, Claude drafts answers.
- Mumsnet Elderly Parents and Gransnet: join as yourself, no links unless asked.
- TikTok/Instagram photo slideshows (hook slide, then the chat, then the app), automated with the social posts.
- WhatsApp Status, testers forwarding to one person each, Trustpilot reviews.
- Directories (AlternativeTo, Product Hunt, BetaList) for SEO links.
- Local press: ChiswickW4.com (weekly newsletter to about 19,500), The Chiswick Calendar, Italian community press.

## Local (Chiswick)
- Free "Bin night?" stickers for people's own wheelie bins (offered on Nextdoor and local groups).
- Pub quiz "reminder round" (£10 bar tab prize per pub, poster on the board).
- Posters on community noticeboards with permission (library, supermarkets, church halls, GP surgeries, pharmacies).
- A6 flyers through letterboxes (outside council leafleting rules; skip "no junk mail").
- Never: stickers on lamp posts or buildings (fly-posting, a crime, on-the-spot fines), anything in Tube stations or trains (TfL permission needed). Street leafleting: check Hounslow's restricted areas first.

## More ideas
- "Check on a neighbour this winter": free Plus November to February for people looking out for an older neighbour; pitch to local press early November.
- Families far from home (diaspora caring for parents abroad); Italian already supported, Spanish next if it lands.
- A LinkedIn founder post from Enrico (optional).
- A free printable weekly medication chart on the blog, with a QR code to set the reminders.
- Clocks go back on 25 October: "I'll remind Mum to change the clocks."
- Get cited by AI assistants: comparison guide, FAQ-rich pages, directory listings, Reddit answers.
- A one-time share prompt right after someone's first "Done".
- Parents of first-year students: the home-safe angle.

## Paid
- Meta click-to-WhatsApp ad for audience 1 from week 3: two images ("Mum's tablets at 9", "Far from Mum and Dad?"), £1 a day for 14 days, **lifetime cap £14**, UK 35 to 65, pre-filled message "Start parents".
- Click-to-WhatsApp opens the chat directly (the Instagram profile doesn't matter); conversations from the ad get 72 hours of free messages.
- Not yet: Google Ads, TikTok ads.

## Six weeks from 12 October 2026
1. Pin launch posts; join groups; Trustpilot asks; WhatsApp Status.
2. Local press emails; order stickers; noticeboards.
3. Meta ad starts; Nextdoor sticker offer; directories.
4. Check the ad after 7 days; pub quiz asks.
5. Letterbox round; re-run the winning ad.
6. Review against targets.

## Targets
By 30 November 2026: 50 accepted setups and 5 Plus trials. Under half of that: change audience or message before spending more.

## Rules
- No prices in ads or posts. Example names: Emily, John, Sam, Mum, Dad. Never imply I'm OK replaces a phone call.
- Spending caps everywhere; never lose money.
