## The seasonal calendar (every year)
Write or refresh these, dated a few days to two weeks before the moment:
- 1st of each month: nothing on the blog (social media covers it).
- Late January: Self Assessment deadline (31 January), new-year habits.
- February: Valentine's (couples, shared admin), half term (teenagers).
- Mothering Sunday (UK, three weeks before Easter; 7 March 2027): staying close to Mum, reminders that matter.
- Clocks go forward (last Sunday of March).
- Easter and the spring bank holidays: travel, checking on parents.
- Summer: heatwaves and older parents (link to NHS heat advice), holidays and travel, exam results (teenagers).
- September: back to school and uni (young people living away), flu jab season starting.
- Clocks go back (last Sunday of October), Halloween, Bonfire Night and fireworks (pets).
- November and December: winter checklist, Christmas planning, older relatives over Christmas, new-year reminders.
